Artists

Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh was a famous Dutch painter known for his unique and colorful style. He was born in 1853 in the Netherlands. Van Gogh created many famous paintings, including The Starry Night and Sunflowers. His artwork is known for its bold colors, expressive brushstrokes, and emotional style. Today, he is considered one of the most influential artists in the history of Western art.

Leonardo da Vinci

Leonardo da Vinci was a famous Italian artist, inventor, and scientist. He was born in 1452 in Vinci, Italy. He is best known for painting the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper. Leonardo studied nature, anatomy, engineering, and many other subjects. He is considered one of the greatest minds and artists of the Renaissance.

Pablo Picasso

Pablo Picasso was a famous Spanish painter and artist. He was born in 1881 in Málaga, Spain. Picasso was one of the founders of the Cubist art movement. He created paintings, sculptures, drawings, and many other artworks. He is considered one of the most influential artists of the 20th century.
